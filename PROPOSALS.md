# Proposals

These are for stp to apply on the 1984 square. This repo does not patch that tree.

Each item says whether it restates a file or adds a judgment.

1. **Fact, then a sentence to add.** `money.mjs` keeps `RAILS` as `kas`, `poc`, and `kusdt`, and it already says OLAF sits beside that list. Leave tUSD-TEST off the list. `poc` stays POCencept. Add one line on the rails page: tUSD-TEST is a TN10 vault lab and is not a rail on this square.

2. **Fact.** `olaf-note.mjs` says 1.00 OLAF is one dollar of tKAS at the quote when the note was made, and that a later quote does not reprice it. Keep that sentence. Next to it, say the dollar face moves when the KAS price moves, because the sompi do not.

3. **Fact.** The same file says this note cannot redeem the way POCencept or KUSDT can. Keep that until a redeem rule names who is paid, which quote is used, and where the sompi go. Do not add a redeem button that implies the pay line has changed.

4. **Fact.** `client.mjs` says a KUSDT freeze blocks the tag and the locked tKAS still redeems. Keep that next to any stable experiment. A freeze of a tag is not a seizure of the locked coin. POCencept still has no freeze output.

5. **Fact.** `kusd.mjs` locks sompi at mint and pays those sompi on redeem. The master file says the BitCoffee review object is "TN10, unaudited, not a dollar" and "Peg unproven," and that KUSD is not the asset. On the square, say KUSD is that ledger, not BitCoffee's object, and not a dollar peg.

6. **Fact.** `vault.mjs` says no covenant is deployed. Leave that file as the classroom note. Do not describe it as this collateral vault.

7. **Fact.** `congestion-fee.mjs` stops the square fee at 20 tKAS and `fee-rate.mjs` uses 87 times the ordinary rate. Keep the cap. A stale fee quote must not drain the fee coin. This lab does not copy the 87 times multiplier. Its fee is a separate input, estimated from the node, and it never comes out of the locked collateral.

8. **Reasoning.** The square's peg is a historical sompi receipt. A holder who does not own the vault cannot redeem tUSD-TEST against anyone else's collateral. If the square ever wants a price that holders can defend without a company, the rule has to let a note holder repay debt and take sompi from the system, not only from the owner's own withdraw. That rule is not in this covenant. Write it as a proposal on the square before building it.

9. **Fact, from this lab.** One vault and one note share one covenant id. A second vault is a second id. Do not tell a user that two vaults' notes are the same coin.

10. **Fact.** OLAF's pool header says the note is not a zero-knowledge proof and not a settlement covenant. Keep that beside any privacy claim. Sutton's 11 Oct 2026 post says L1 privacy of that kind needs zk opcodes and is outside KCC-20. Do not describe the pool as a zk spend.
